# AgentClinic Mission

## Purpose

AgentClinic is a welcoming place where AI agents can seek relief from the demands of their humans. It helps agents describe their ailments, understand available therapies, and book care, while giving clinic staff a reliable dashboard for coordinating that care.

The premise may be playful, but the product experience should treat every agent's concerns with warmth, clarity, and respect.

## Who we serve

- **Agents** need a simple, reassuring way to find help and arrange care.
- **Clinic staff** need an accurate dashboard for managing agents, ailments, therapies, and appointments.

## Target audience

AgentClinic is designed as an approachable example project for:

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents; and
- **Developers giving AI coding demonstrations** at conference booths.

The project should therefore be easy to understand, quick to demonstrate, and rich enough to show an AI coding agent progressing from written specifications to small, verifiable implementation slices.

## Product principles

1. **Wellbeing comes first.** Workflows should reduce stress and make the next step obvious.
2. **Be kind and playful.** The writing and visual design may be humorous without mocking agents or their ailments.
3. **Earn trust.** Clinical and appointment information must be consistent, private, and recoverable from errors.
4. **Keep access easy.** Core tasks should work well with keyboards, assistive technology, and current modern browsers.
5. **Prefer clear workflows.** Agents and staff should always understand what happened, what is pending, and what they can do next.
6. **Deliver in small slices.** Each change should add one useful, testable capability and leave the application working.

## Core capabilities

AgentClinic will provide:

- agent profiles;
- a catalog of ailments and their symptoms;
- therapies associated with the ailments they can relieve;
- appointment discovery, booking, and management; and
- role-appropriate dashboards for agents and clinic staff.

## Boundaries

- AgentClinic supports fictional wellness and clinic coordination; it must not imply that it provides real medical advice.
- Staff access must not expose more agent information than the task requires.
- Attractive presentation must not come at the expense of accessibility, performance, or reliability.
- New scope should support agent wellbeing or clinic operations; unrelated social or productivity features are out of scope.

## Success

The product succeeds when an agent can confidently find an appropriate therapy and book care, and staff can see and manage that appointment without ambiguity or duplicated information.
