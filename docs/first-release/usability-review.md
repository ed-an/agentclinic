## Scripts

### Agent journey

1. Find an Ailment and understand its symptoms.
2. Find a relevant Therapy and available time.
3. Book care and understand confirmation.
4. Sign in, review the dashboard, and cancel an eligible Appointment.
5. Recover from one scripted validation or session-expiry state.

### Staff journey

1. Sign in and locate the Appointment in the filtered queue.
2. Confirm an eligible Appointment and verify the Agent-visible result.
3. Cancel an eligible Appointment and understand its recorded state.
4. Recover from one scripted stale/conflicting action.
